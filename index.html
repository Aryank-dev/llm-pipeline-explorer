Why Cosine Similarity Matters in Transformers
In modern LLMs (GPT, LLaMA, Claude), the Self-Attention mechanism computes attention weights via scaled dot products: Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(Q Kᵀ / √dₖ) V. Sentences or tokens that point in similar directions in latent space receive higher attention weights!
